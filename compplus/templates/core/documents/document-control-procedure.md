# Document and Record Control Procedure

**Owner:** {{ roles.system_manager }}  
**Approver:** {{ roles.executive_owner }}  
**Review cycle:** Annual

## 1. Purpose

{{ organization.legal_name }} controls documents and records so staff use current, approved information and can demonstrate what happened, when and by whom.

## 2. What is controlled

This procedure applies to policies, procedures, plans, registers, forms, meeting minutes, reports, evidence and externally supplied documents used by the management system.

## 3. Document identification

Every controlled document must show:

- title;
- owner;
- approver;
- version;
- effective date;
- review date;
- status: draft, approved, retired or archived.

CompPlus generates these fields automatically where possible.

## 4. Drafting and review

1. The owner creates or updates the document from an approved CompPlus template.
2. Highlighted fields are completed or confirmed.
3. Relevant reviewers check accuracy, practicality and legal or contractual requirements.
4. Comments are resolved before approval.

## 5. Approval and publication

Only an authorised approver may publish a controlled version. Approved documents are stored in:

{{ document.storage_location | confirm }}

Staff are directed to the current controlled copy. Uncontrolled downloads or printed copies must be treated as reference copies only.

## 6. Change control

Material changes require a new version and change summary. CompPlus retains previous approved versions and the approval history.

## 7. External documents

Applicable laws, standards, customer requirements, supplier instructions and regulator guidance are recorded in the Legal Requirements Register. The assigned owner checks for changes at the defined review frequency.

## 8. Retention and disposal

Records are retained for the period defined in the retention schedule or applicable legal, contractual and business requirements. Disposal must be secure and recorded where the information is sensitive.

## 9. Access and protection

Access is based on role and business need. Sensitive records must be protected against unauthorised access, alteration, loss and premature destruction.

## 11. Exceptions

Any exception must be documented, risk-assessed, approved by {{ roles.system_manager }} and given an expiry or review date.
