# Competence, Awareness and Training Procedure

**Owner:** {{ roles.system_manager }}  
**Review cycle:** Annual

## 1. Purpose

{{ organization.legal_name }} ensures that people doing work under its control are competent and understand the policies, risks, responsibilities and consequences relevant to their role.

## 2. Training needs

Training needs are identified from job responsibilities, selected compliance packs, risk assessments, incidents, audit findings, legal requirements and changes to systems or processes.

## 3. Minimum training

All workers receive:

- induction covering key policies, reporting routes and acceptable behaviour;
- role-specific training before carrying out higher-risk activities;
- refresher training at the following frequency: {{ training.frequency | confirm }};
- additional training following important changes, incidents or recurring errors.

## 4. Competence records

The training register records the person, role, required learning, completion date, result, evidence, refresher date and any follow-up action.

## 5. Effectiveness

Training effectiveness may be checked through quizzes, practical observation, simulated exercises, audit results, incident trends or manager confirmation. Completion alone does not prove competence where practical performance is required.

## 6. Overdue training

CompPlus sends reminders before due dates. Overdue mandatory training is escalated to the worker’s manager and {{ roles.system_manager }}.
