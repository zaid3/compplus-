# Nonconformity and Corrective Action Procedure

**Owner:** {{ roles.system_manager }}  
**Review cycle:** Annual

## 1. Purpose

{{ organization.legal_name }} records and corrects problems so that immediate harm is controlled, root causes are addressed and the same issue is less likely to recur.

## 2. Sources of corrective action

A record may arise from an incident, complaint, failed objective, audit finding, control failure, legal non-compliance, supplier issue, monitoring result or management review decision.

## 3. Required workflow

1. Record the problem and available evidence.
2. Take immediate containment or correction action.
3. Assess impact, risk and whether similar problems exist elsewhere.
4. Identify root cause using a proportionate method such as five-whys, causal analysis or process review.
5. Define corrective actions, owners and deadlines.
6. Complete and evidence each action.
7. Verify effectiveness after enough time has passed.
8. Close with approval or reopen if ineffective.

## 5. Timeframes

- Immediate containment: as soon as reasonably possible.
- Action plan: normally within 10 working days.
- Completion: based on risk and complexity.
- Effectiveness check: after implementation and before closure.

## 6. Records

The corrective-action register records the issue, source, impact, root cause, containment, actions, owners, deadlines, evidence, effectiveness result and closure approval.
